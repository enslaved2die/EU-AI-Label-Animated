# EU AI Label — Animated

![Animation](Animation.gif)

Animated version of the official [EU icons for labelling AI-generated content](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content). 
The EU icons help disclose AI-generated or manipulated content ("deepfakes") and AI-generated text, in line with Article 50 AI Act transparency rules. They come in Basic, Fully AI-Generated, and Partially AI-Modified variants.

Animated in After Effects for use with After Effects and Premiere Pro
